## 4. Cars — 🔴 READ BEFORE BOOKING ANYTHING

**I could not obtain live quotes** (rental sites unreachable). But the research surfaced something more important than price: **this leg may not be legal for your group at all.**

### Three stacked blockers

**1. Minimum age is 21.** Most Italian rental companies set the floor at **21**, and normally require the licence to have been held **at least one year**. You describe the group as "aged 20–21". **Every 20-year-old is excluded outright**, and any 21-year-old who got their licence recently may also fail the held-one-year test.

**2. Under-25 surcharge applies to all of you.** Verified per-day ranges, charged *per driver*:

| Company | Young-driver surcharge (under 25) |
|---|---|
| Hertz | ~€25/day |
| Europcar | ~€20/day |
| Sixt | ~€30/day |
| General market range | €15–€30/day |

Across 2 cars × 3 days, that is roughly **€90–€180 per car** in surcharge alone, before the rental itself — and more if you register second drivers.

**3. The 9-seater van is the usual killer — but maybe not here.** Contrary to the common assumption, sources indicate Italian suppliers **do generally rent 9-seaters from 21**, with the under-25 surcharge applied (Maggiore explicitly states 21 + a credit card in the driver's own name). Typical 9-seat models: Mercedes Vito, Ford Tourneo, Toyota Proace Verso, Fiat Ducato. **However**, the *"minimum age jumps to 25"* rule does bite on **luxury and specialty categories** — and some suppliers classify large vans that way. **This must be confirmed with the specific supplier before you rely on it.** I could not verify it per-company.

### Other confirmed points

- **Automatic transmission is scarce and expensive in Italy**, and is realistically only found at airport locations. FLR pickup is the right choice for that reason. **Book automatic explicitly and early** — do not assume.
- **One-way FLR → Rome fees are real but wildly inconsistent** in reported experience (anywhere from ~€11 to ~€70, some report none). I found **no verifiable current figure** for Hertz FLR→Rome. Get it in writing in the quote.

### What to do

Quote all of these for **FLR pickup 26 Oct → Rome drop 28 Oct**, and on each quote confirm *in writing*: minimum age, under-25 surcharge per day, one-way fee, automatic availability, and second-driver cost.

- Hertz: https://www.hertz.com
- Sixt: https://www.sixt.com/car-rental/italy/
- Europcar: https://www.europcar.com
- Maggiore (Italian, explicit 21+ policy): https://www.maggiore.it
- Auto Europe (broker, surfaces age rules up front): https://www.autoeurope.com

> **Strong recommendation:** price the **9-seater van driven by a single qualifying driver** against the 2-car option. One van means **one** young-driver surcharge instead of two-to-four, one one-way fee instead of two, and one parking space in Val d'Orcia instead of two. If exactly one person in the group is 21 with a licence held over a year, the van is not just cheaper — it may be your **only** legal configuration.

## 9. International Driving Permit while abroad — 🔴 THIS GATES §4

**The rule:** an IDP that accompanies a US driving licence **can only be issued in the United States**. No other country can issue one against a US licence. There is no Paris workaround, no embassy service, no same-day option in Europe.

**Italy legally requires it.** Under **DLGS 285/1992 Art. 135**, non-EU licence holders must carry an IDP alongside their national licence. Driving without one risks a **€408–€1,634 fine**, and — far worse for eight students — **your insurance may be void in an accident**. Hertz, Avis, Europcar, Sixt, Enterprise, Budget and Maggiore will all refuse pickup at most Italian airports without one.

**Can you still get one from Paris? Yes — just.**

AAA accepts remote applications and ships internationally at your expense:

| Step | Time / cost |
|---|---|
| Processing | **5 business days** |
| International shipping | **3–10 days** |
| Permit fee | **$20** |
| Photo fee | **$10** |
| Shipping | **$9 – $220+** depending on destination |
| **Realistic total** | **~8–15 days door to door** |
| AAA's own advice | Apply **2+ weeks** before departure |

You need it by **26 Oct**. From 7 Sept that is 49 days — **comfortable, but only if you start now.** Every driver needs their own; you cannot share one.

Two routes: apply online at AAA and ship to Paris, or mail a completed form with two signed passport photos, a photocopy of both sides of the licence and $20 to **AAA/IDP, 1000 AAA Dr., Heathrow, FL 32746, Attn: Mailstop #28**.

- Apply online: https://shop.cluballiance.aaa.com/products/international-driving-permit-apply-online
- AATA is the only other US-authorised issuer.

> ⚠️ **Beware third-party "instant IDP" sites.** Several appeared in search results offering immediate digital permits. Only **AAA** and **AATA** are authorised to issue US IDPs. A digital-only "IDP" is not valid — the real document is a physical booklet, and Italian police and rental desks want the booklet.

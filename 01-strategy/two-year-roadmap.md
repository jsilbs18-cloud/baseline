# Two-Year Buildout Roadmap

Four phases. Each phase has an organizational objective (what the team becomes),
deal objectives (what it proves), and system objectives (what gets built). Dates are
relative to "T0" = ED sign-off on the mandate document.

---

## Phase 0 — Codify (T0 → T+60 days)

**Theme: write down how we work before hiring more people to work.**

- Ratify mandate, boundaries, and decision rights (`01-strategy/mandate-and-mission.md`)
- Adopt the deal lifecycle and stage gates; retrofit all active deals into stages
- Convert existing one-pagers to the new template; every active deal gets a
  structured deal record (`04-templates/deal-record-schema.yaml`)
- Score all active deals with the strategic importance framework; produce the first
  tiered pipeline view for the Secretary
- Stand up the weekly pipeline meeting and the monthly Secretary review
- Inventory current staff against the role cards; identify the 3–4 most urgent gaps

**Exit test:** the ED can pull up one page showing every deal, its tier, its stage,
its owner, and its next decision — and it's current.

## Phase 1 — Prove (T+2 → T+8 months)

**Theme: deliver flagship deals with the new machine; hire the core.**

- Close or publicly announce 2–3 Tier 1 deals run fully through the new process
- Hire wave 1 (see `02-organization/roles-and-hiring.md`): deal execution leads,
  head of the government toolkit function, permitting lead
- Publish the internal federal toolkit playbook (`06-toolkit/`) and train all deal
  teams on it — every deal team should know every instrument available
- Build the origination coverage model: every channel has a named owner
- Stand up the Codex/CUI docs-as-code environment (`07-systems/codex-workspace-plan.md`);
  one-pagers and pipeline dashboards generate from deal records

**Exit test:** a new deal can go from intake to a Secretary-ready package memo in
under 30 days without heroics.

## Phase 2 — Scale (T+8 → T+16 months)

**Theme: from deals to a portfolio; from inbound to origination.**

- Hire wave 2: sector leads for each priority sector; portfolio management function
- Run the sector screening process top-down: for each priority sector, a chokepoint
  map and a target list — origination stops being purely reactive
- Formalize the interagency network: standing counterparts named at DOE, DoD, Treasury,
  EXIM, DOT, USDA, the Permitting Council, and key states
- Portfolio management operational: every closed deal has milestones, monitoring,
  and a named steward; first portfolio review to the Secretary
- Measurement live: capital facilitated, leverage ratio, cycle times reported quarterly

**Exit test:** at least one closed deal originated top-down from sector screening
(not inbound), and a quarterly portfolio report exists.

## Phase 3 — Institutionalize (T+16 → T+24 months)

**Theme: make it durable beyond any one person or administration moment.**

- Full org at target size; succession/backup named for every critical role
- Playbooks complete: every stage of the lifecycle has a written procedure a new
  hire can execute
- Knowledge base mature: precedent library of closed deal structures, term sheets,
  and federal packages (in the CUI environment) so new deals start from precedent
- Decision on permanence: legislative proposal, or executive continuity plan
- Second-generation systems: automated Secretary briefing book, portfolio analytics

**Exit test:** the ED could hand the office to a successor with a two-week
transition and no loss of deal momentum.

## Metrics dashboard (tracked from Phase 0, reported from Phase 1)

| Metric | Definition | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Capital facilitated | Private $ closed/announced with Accelerator role | Quarterly |
| Federal leverage | Private $ per federal $ committed (by instrument) | Quarterly |
| Pipeline depth | Deals by tier and stage; aging per stage | Weekly |
| Cycle time | Intake → package memo; package → announcement | Monthly |
| Permitting delta | Estimated time saved vs. baseline process | Per deal |
| Portfolio health | % milestones on track; status of gov't positions | Quarterly |
| Sector coverage | Priority sectors with active deal or explicit no-play | Quarterly |

## Risks to the buildout itself

- **Deal gravity** — deals will always feel more urgent than building the machine;
  Phase 0 fails without protected staff time. Mitigation: chief of staff owns the
  buildout as their primary deliverable.
- **Hiring friction** — federal hiring timelines can eat a year. Mitigation: lead
  with detailees and IPAs while permanent hiring runs in parallel.
- **Mandate creep** — every agency will route "investment things" here. Mitigation:
  written boundaries + a referral path so "no" still lands somewhere useful.
- **Key-person risk** — early phases concentrate knowledge in few heads. Mitigation:
  the docs-as-code discipline in `07-systems/` is the antidote; enforce it.

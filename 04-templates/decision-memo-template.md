# Decision Memo — Template

For any decision that exceeds the one-pager: package approval, tier change on a
Tier 1 deal, kill recommendation, terms involving equity or special rights. Two
pages maximum; options are real options with honest costs.

```
[CUI MARKING BLOCK]

MEMORANDUM FOR: <ED / Secretary>
FROM:           <deal lead>, through <Head of Deals>
DATE:           <machine-stamped>
RE:             <Deal codename> — <decision requested>
DECISION BY:    <date> (<why this date is real — investor board, statutory
                deadline, competing jurisdiction's offer expiry>)

1. DECISION REQUESTED
<One sentence. Approve/select/authorize what.>

2. BACKGROUND
<One short paragraph. Assume the reader saw the one-pager; do not re-litigate
the deal. Only what changed or what the reader must know to decide.>

3. OPTIONS
Option A — <name>
  Package: <instruments, agencies, amounts>
  Federal cost / exposure: <$>      Leverage: <ratio>
  Pros: <2 bullets>   Cons: <2 bullets, honest>

Option B — <name>
  <same structure>

Option C — Do not proceed / refer
  <what happens to the deal and the underlying strategic need>

4. RECOMMENDATION
<Which option and the one reason that matters most.>

5. RISKS OF RECOMMENDED OPTION
<Top 3, each with mitigation. Include political/appearance risk explicitly.>

6. INTERAGENCY POSITIONS
<Each agency whose instrument is used: supports / supports with conditions /
objects — one line each. No silent objections discovered later.>

7. APPROVALS
Head of Deals: ___   Head of Toolkit: ___   Counsel: ___
ED: APPROVE / DISAPPROVE / DISCUSS        Secretary (if req'd): ___
```

## Rules

1. **The "decision by" date must be externally real.** Manufactured urgency burns
   credibility with the front office exactly once.
2. **Option C is always present.** If declining is unthinkable, the memo should
   show why rather than omit it.
3. **Interagency positions are collected before the memo goes up**, not after.
4. **Counsel signs before the ED sees it** when terms involve equity, special
   rights, or novel instrument use.
